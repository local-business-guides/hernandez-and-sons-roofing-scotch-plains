A simple way to compare contractors

Start with the same questions for each contractor you speak with. That makes it easier to compare what’s included and spot information you still need. Have your notes ready, but don’t feel you need to know the technical cause of a problem before asking for help.

Use this checklist:
- Explain what you noticed, where it is, and when you first saw it.
- Ask how the roof will be assessed and what the recommendation is based on.
- Request a clear description of the proposed work and what it includes.
- Ask how questions or updates will be handled.
- Take time to read the details before deciding.

Avoid treating a confident sales pitch as a substitute for clear information. Don’t compare proposals by a headline or a short summary if the work described is different. And don’t attempt a roof inspection yourself. If you can’t safely see an area, tell the contractor what you’ve noticed from the ground.
