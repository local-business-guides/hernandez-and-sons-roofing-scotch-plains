A simple hiring checklist

A little preparation can make it easier to compare conversations with roofing contractors. Keep your questions in one place, and don't feel you need to identify the technical cause before asking for help. You can share what you've observed and ask what should be checked.

Consider asking:

  • What information do you need about the roof concern?
  • What parts of the roof would you assess?
  • What work are you proposing, and what does that scope include?
  • What should I expect as the next step after this discussion?
  • Who should I contact if I need clarification?

Avoid assuming that a quick description of the problem is the same as a full explanation of the work. Also avoid agreeing to a scope you don't understand. Take notes, compare the explanations, and ask follow-up questions if the answers leave something unresolved.
